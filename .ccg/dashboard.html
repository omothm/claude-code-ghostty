Fleet idle on you is the north star: of your active time (busy stretches plus the 1–10 min gaps between them), the share when no session was working and no background agent was running — you were reviewing, deciding or away for a moment and the whole fleet waited. Gaps over 10 min count as breaks, not idle (set fleetBreakMin in config.json to change it). Close the red gaps by queuing the next task in another session before you sit down to review a finished one. Concurrent share and agent-hours are the payoff: more of your busy time with ≥2 sessions running, and more total session-time worked per day. Deltas compare the last 24h to the median of your previous 7 active days. Background agents (☕️) count as busy; watching (👀) and idle don't.
